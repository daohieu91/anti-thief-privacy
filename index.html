Privacy Policy — ThiefSnap

Last updated: June 12, 2026

The short version. ThiefSnap protects your own phone. Everything it captures — your PIN, settings, and intruder selfies — stays on your device by default and is deleted when you uninstall the app. The microphone is only used, on-device, to hear a clap or whistle when you start “Find my phone”; audio is never recorded or sent anywhere. The only data that ever leaves your device is for ads (Free version, via Google AdMob); upgrading to Pro removes that too. Intruder selfies stay on your phone and are never uploaded. We never track other people. Questions: hieu.dtvt10@gmail.com.

1. Who we are

  • App: ThiefSnap (package com.essenty.thiefalarm).
  • Developer: Essenty Dev Team, based in Vietnam.
  • Contact: hieu.dtvt10@gmail.com.
  • The app has no account and no sign-up.

2. What the app does

ThiefSnap sounds a loud alarm when your phone is moved, taken from your pocket, unplugged, or its headphones/Wi-Fi are changed while protection is on; it can capture a selfie of whoever sets off the alarm (and on a wrong PIN), saved on your device; and it can ring on a clap or whistle to help you find a misplaced phone. It is designed to protect the owner’s own device.

3. Data we handle

3.1 On your device only (never sent to us)

  • Your disarm PIN, stored only as a salted hash — never in plain text, never transmitted.
  • App settings (sensitivity, volume, enabled detectors, theme, language, chosen alarm sound).
  • Intruder selfies and the intrusion history (time + trigger), stored in the app’s private storage — never uploaded.

All of the above is removed when you uninstall the app.

3.2 Microphone (clap / whistle)

When you start “Find my phone”, the app listens through the microphone on the device only to detect a clap or whistle. The audio is analysed in memory and immediately discarded. No audio is recorded, stored, or transmitted. Listening stops when you stop it or after a short timeout.

3.3 Ads (Free version)

The free version shows banner ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use a device advertising identifier (AD_ID) and related data to serve and measure ads, as described in Google’s privacy policy. Upgrading to Pro removes ads. See Google’s policy linked in section 6.

3.4 Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment and tells the app whether you own Pro. We do not receive your payment details.

3.5 What we do NOT collect

  • No user accounts and no personal profile.
  • No location data.
  • No contacts, SMS, or call logs.
  • No stored or transmitted audio recordings.
  • No tracking of any person other than protecting your own device.

  • On your device: PIN hash, settings, selfies, history (sections 3.1).
  • To Google: ads (AdMob) and purchase state (Play Billing).
  • Nowhere else. Selfies and history never leave your device.

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and portability. Legal bases: performing the purchase contract (Pro), and your consent (ads). You may complain to your local Data Protection Authority.

7.4 California (CCPA/CPRA)

We do not sell or “share” your personal information. You have the rights to know, to delete, and to non-discrimination. The advertising ID used by AdMob may be treated as personal information; you can limit it in Android settings or remove ads by upgrading to Pro.

8. Children’s privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect children’s personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us.

9. Data retention & security

On-device data lives only on your device until you delete it or uninstall. The PIN is stored as a salted hash. Intruder selfies are stored only in the app’s private storage and are never uploaded.
